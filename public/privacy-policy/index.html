1. Introduction

AP Creative Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides professional resume design, portfolio websites, posters, and related creative services. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, contact us on WhatsApp, email us, or submit our Google Form.

By using our website or contacting us about our services, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please do not share personal information with us.

2. Information We Collect

This website does not run its own account system or store form submissions on our servers. Personal information reaches us only when you choose to share it, for example through:

  • WhatsApp messages to our business number
  • Our Google Form enquiry link
  • Email to our contact address

Depending on what you send, that may include:

  • Name and preferred contact details (email, phone / WhatsApp number)
  • Education, skills, work experience, projects, and career goals
  • Photographs or other images you provide for a portfolio or design
  • Existing resumes, drafts, or project files you share for redesign
  • Service preference, deadlines, and notes about your requirements

When you visit the website, our hosting provider may process standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and request logs needed to serve the pages securely. We do not ask for passwords, bank or card numbers, government ID numbers, or other highly sensitive credentials, and you should not send them to us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share only to:

  • Respond to enquiries and discuss scope, pricing, and timelines
  • Create and deliver the services you request (for example resumes, portfolio sites, or posters)
  • Communicate about revisions and delivery
  • Handle follow-up questions related to your project

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your details for unrelated marketing lists. If we ever wish to show your completed work as a sample on our website or social channels, we will ask for your permission first.

4. AI Tools and Third-Party Services

We may use everyday productivity and design tools to prepare your deliverables. Where those tools process content you provided, we use them only for completing your project.

Third-party services involved when you contact us or visit the site include:

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — messages you send are processed under Meta’s privacy terms
  • Google Forms — responses you submit are processed under Google’s privacy terms
  • Vercel — hosts this website and may process standard access logs

We do not embed third-party advertising trackers on this site. Fonts, scripts, and images used on the site are served from our own domain where possible.

5. How We Share Information

We share personal information only when needed to run the service you asked for, for example:

  • With the platform you used to contact us (WhatsApp or Google), as part of that product
  • With team members working on your project, on a need-to-know basis
  • If required by law or a valid legal request

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us, including limiting access to project details to people involved in delivery, and using the security features of the platforms we communicate through (such as WhatsApp and email).

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please avoid sending highly sensitive documents (IDs, financial credentials, passwords) over chat or forms. If you believe your information has been misused in connection with our services, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep project-related information only as long as needed to complete your work, handle agreed revisions, and answer reasonable follow-up questions. After that, we aim to delete or de-identify materials we no longer need.

Messages and files may also remain in WhatsApp, Google Forms, or email systems according to those providers’ own retention settings. You can ask us to delete information we hold about your project by contacting us (see Contact section). We will respond within a reasonable time, subject to any legal obligations to retain records.

8. Cookies and Analytics

This website is primarily static. We do not currently run a separate advertising or analytics cookie banner for third-party marketing trackers. Our hosting provider may use technical logs or cookies required to operate and secure the site.

If we introduce optional analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, provide appropriate notice or controls.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Consent Withdrawal

Depending on applicable law where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about your enquiry or project
  • Request correction of inaccurate details
  • Request deletion of information we no longer need to keep
  • Withdraw consent for optional uses (for example showing your work as a public sample)

To exercise these rights, email us at the address below. We may need to verify that the request comes from you before making changes.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our services are aimed at students, freshers, professionals, and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for our own marketing. If a parent or guardian believes a child has shared personal information with us inappropriately, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

We will review privacy-related messages and respond as promptly as we reasonably can.

12. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices, tools, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published. Continued use of the website or continued contact with us after an update means you should review the revised policy.

This page is provided for transparency about how AP Creative Studio handles information shared in the course of enquiries and projects. It is not legal advice.
