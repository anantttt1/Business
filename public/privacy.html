Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AP Creative Studio (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what happens to the information you share when you contact us.

What we collect

This website itself does not store your details. Information reaches us only when you choose to message us on WhatsApp or submit our Google Form. That may include your name, email, WhatsApp number, service preference, deadline and the details you write about your requirements.

How we use it

We use it only to reply to you, agree scope, deliver your work and improve our service. We do not sell your personal data.

Third-party services

WhatsApp (Meta) and Google Forms (Google) process the information you send through them under their own privacy policies. This site is hosted on Vercel and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your IP address is visible to those providers when you visit.

Please do not share sensitive documents

Do not send ID numbers, bank or card details, or passwords in the form or chat. We will never ask for them.

Retention and your choices

We keep project details only as long as needed to complete your work and handle follow-up questions. You can ask us to correct or delete your details at apcreativestudio0@gmail.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, email apcreativestudio0@gmail.com.
